Help

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose my size?

Sizes run from XS to XXL depending on the item. Each product window shows the sizes available. If you are between two sizes, we recommend the larger one. You can also message us on WhatsApp for help.

How long does delivery take?

Accra usually takes 1 to 2 working days. Other regions usually take 2 to 5 working days. Delivery is free on orders over GH₵ 400.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes. You can exchange unworn items with their tags attached within 7 days of delivery.

How do I pay?

You can pay by Mobile Money or pay on delivery in Accra. In this demo, no real payment is taken.

Can I order a single item on WhatsApp?

Yes. Open any product and tap "Order this on WhatsApp" to send that item straight to us.
